# New York City / New York State Housing Market Knowledge

## NYC Market Overview (2025)

### Median Home Prices
- **NYC citywide**: $750K-$875K (varies by source/borough)
- **Manhattan**: $1.0M-$1.05M median
- **Brooklyn**: ~$800K+ (30+ neighborhoods over $1M median)
- **Queens**: Rising due to condo boom
- **Bronx**: Lower, seeing growth
- **Staten Island**: $730K-$750K (up 4-5%)

### Market Characteristics (October 2025)
- **Under contract**: 2,191 homes (up 10.4% year-over-year)
- **Median asking price**: $1.05M (stable year-over-year)
- **Sale-to-list ratio**: 97.9% (2.1% average discount)
- **Days on market**: 62 days (increasing)
- **Sales volume**: Up 12.9% year-over-year
- **Price per square foot**: $871 (down 1.7% year-over-year)

### New York State
- **Statewide median**: $490K-$561K
- **NYC influence**: Heavily skews state average upward
- **Upstate markets**: Syracuse, Kingston, Rochester (5-6% projected gains)

### Price Comparison
- **NYC**: 127-203% higher than national average (depending on source/neighborhood)
- **Overall cost of living**: Significantly above national average

## Market Dynamics

### Current State (2025)
- **Balance**: More balanced than pandemic years
- **Normalization**: After volatility of 2020-2024
- **Price growth**: Moderating to single-digit sustainable pace
- **Inventory**: Tight, down 9% from Q1 2024
- **Activity**: Strong sustained buyer interest

### Seller Pricing Strategy
- **Adaptability**: Sellers pricing thoughtfully
- **Not overpricing**: Avoiding steep cuts later
- **Strategic**: Aiming to attract buyers without leaving money on table
- **Flexibility**: More negotiation in some premium neighborhoods

### Buyer Behavior
- **Selectivity**: More deliberate than pandemic years
- **Adjustment**: Adapting budgets to higher rates
- **Negotiation**: Harder bargaining
- **Waiting**: Some on sidelines hoping for rate drops

### Competition Level
- **Sales above list**: 39.2% (down 0.71 points year-over-year)
- **Price drops**: 15.1% of homes (up from 13.0%)
- **Sale-to-list**: 100.2% (down 0.32 points)
- **Multiple offers**: Still occurring for well-priced homes
- **Not frenzied**: Not pandemic-era bidding wars
