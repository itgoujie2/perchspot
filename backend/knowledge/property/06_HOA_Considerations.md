# HOA Considerations

## Small Community HOA Fees
@applies_when: has_hoa=true
@priority: high

HOA fees for small communities (20-30 homes) can reach $300-400/month when professional management companies handle administration for limited unit counts. The per-unit cost is higher because fixed expenses (management fees, insurance, landscaping contracts) are divided among fewer owners.

---

## HOA Reserve Fund Assessment
@applies_when: has_hoa=true
@priority: high

Well-managed HOAs maintain reserves of at least 70% funded for major repairs. Request the HOA's reserve study and financial statements before purchasing. Underfunded reserves often lead to special assessments of $5,000-$20,000+ per unit for roof replacements, siding repairs, or infrastructure updates.

---

## HOA Budget Review
@applies_when: has_hoa=true
@priority: medium

Review the HOA budget for these red flags: reserves below 50% funded, recent special assessments, pending litigation, deferred maintenance items, or rapidly increasing dues (more than 5% annually). Request meeting minutes from the past year to understand ongoing issues.

---

## HOA Insurance Coverage
@applies_when: has_hoa=true
@priority: medium

Understand what the HOA master insurance policy covers vs. what you need individual HO-6 (condo) or walls-in coverage for. Common areas are typically covered by HOA policy, but your personal property, fixtures, and improvements require separate coverage.

---

## HOA Rental Restrictions
@applies_when: has_hoa=true
@priority: medium

Many HOAs restrict rentals - some cap the percentage of units that can be rented, others prohibit short-term rentals entirely, and some require owner-occupancy for the first year. These restrictions can significantly impact investment property potential and resale value to investors.

---

## HOA Special Assessment Risk
@applies_when: has_hoa=true
@priority: high

Special assessments can be levied for unexpected repairs or underfunded reserves. Ask about any planned or pending special assessments. Major building systems (roof, siding, plumbing, elevators) typically need replacement every 20-30 years - check when these were last updated.

---

## High HOA Fee Evaluation
@applies_when: has_hoa=true
@applies_when: hoa_fee>300
@priority: high

Monthly HOA fees above $300 warrant careful evaluation. Verify what's included - some high fees cover utilities, internet, landscaping, amenities, and insurance that you'd pay separately in a non-HOA property. Calculate the true cost comparison.

---

## Very High HOA Fee Warning
@applies_when: has_hoa=true
@applies_when: hoa_fee>500
@priority: high

Monthly HOA fees above $500 are significant - this adds $6,000+ annually to housing costs. Ensure the fee is justified by included amenities, services, or location. High fees can impact resale as they narrow the pool of qualified buyers.
