# Seattle Housing Market Knowledge

## Market Overview (2025-2026)

### Median Home Prices
- **Seattle citywide**: $790K-$898K (varying by source and time)
- **Redfin December 2025**: $790K (down 0.63% year-over-year)
- **Zillow**: $847,975 (down 1.7% year-over-year)
- **Typical range**: $850K-$900K depending on neighborhood and property type

### Market Characteristics
- **Competition level**: Very competitive (73 out of 100 score)
- **Days on market**: 10-17 days average
- **Sale-to-list price**: Hot homes sell approximately 1% above list price
- **Market status**: Functioning balanced market (not overheated, not collapsed)
- **Inventory**: 3.5 months supply (most functional level in years)
- **Sales activity intensity**: 32% (healthy for January cycle)

### Price Comparison to National Average
- **Seattle median**: 84% higher than US national average
- **Cost of living**: 45% higher than national average

## Eastside vs Seattle Proper

### "Eastside" Definition
Cities east of Lake Washington:
- Bellevue
- Kirkland
- Redmond
- Issaquah
- Sammamish
- Bothell

### Eastside Characteristics
- **Tech industry concentration**: Microsoft, Amazon offices, startups
- **Higher cost of living**: Generally more expensive than Seattle proper
- **All Eastside cities with $1M+ median**: 6 cities exceed $1M median
- **School quality**: Generally highly-rated districts
- **Newer development**: More modern construction than Seattle

### Seattle Proper Characteristics
- **Urban core**: Dense, walkable neighborhoods
- **More variety**: Greater price range and diversity
- **Older housing stock**: Mix of historic and modern
- **Transit access**: Better public transportation
- **Cultural amenities**: More restaurants, arts, entertainment

## Market Dynamics by Price Segment

### Premium ($1.5M+)
- **Insulation from economic pressures**: Buyers less affected by mortgage rates
- **Cash buyers common**: Large down payments typical
- **Single-family homes**: Up 8% year-over-year
- **Luxury neighborhoods**: Pacific Heights-style areas holding strong
- **Condition critical**: Turnkey homes move quickly, dated homes negotiate harder

### Mid-Range ($700K-$1.5M)
- **Highest sales activity**: Strong demand, limited inventory
- **New Year Kickoff effect**: Competitive conditions, dynamic opportunities
- **Mortgage rate sensitive**: Some affordability challenges
- **Quality matters**: Updated homes outperform dated ones significantly

### Entry-Level (Under $700K)
- **Condos dominate**: Especially under $550K
- **Affordability ceiling**: Payment shock most keenly felt
- **Newer buildings move faster**: HOA scrutiny high
- **Longer negotiations**: More concessions than upper tiers

## Market Trends & Forecast

### Current Trends (2025-2026)
- **Stabilization**: No rapid escalation, modest near-flat growth
- **Balanced market emerging**: Inventory and activity aligning by price band
- **Selectivity increasing**: Buyers choosing carefully, not rushing
- **Micro-market separation**: Outcomes depend on neighborhood, type, execution

### Price Forecasts
- **Zillow 1-year projection** (Oct 2025-Oct 2026): Near-flat to slight appreciation
- **Lagging smaller WA markets**: Moses Lake, Mount Vernon seeing higher appreciation
- **Why Seattle lags**: Affordability ceiling reached, saturation in premium segments

### Inventory Trends
- **3.5 months supply**: Most functional in years
- **Active listings increasing**: Buyers have more options
- **Premium neighborhoods**: Still require pricing discipline
- **New listings**: Generally declining even as market heats up

## Buyer vs Seller Market Dynamics

### Seller Advantages
- **Low inventory**: Still favors sellers in most segments
- **Multiple offers**: Common in well-priced homes
- **Quick sales**: 10-17 days average in hot neighborhoods
- **Above-list sales**: Premium properties command bidding wars

### Buyer Advantages
- **More functional market**: Better visibility and options than recent years
- **Less urgency**: Not forced into immediate decisions
- **Negotiation possible**: Especially for dated homes or condos
- **Price stabilization**: Less risk of rapid appreciation after purchase

### Balanced Market Indicators
- **Sales activity**: 32% intensity (healthy but not frenzied)
- **Inventory growth**: Usable supply finally available
- **Selectivity**: Competition no longer automatic
- **Days on market**: Slightly longer than peak periods

---

## Mortgage Rate Impact

### Current Environment (2025-2026)
- **Rates**: Low 6% range
- **Impact**: Enhanced affordability vs 7%+ rates
- **Buyer behavior**: Shift from reactive to intentional
- **Demand**: Stayed strong, supply became usable

### Rate Sensitivity by Segment
- **Premium buyers**: Less rate-sensitive (cash/large down payments)
- **Mid-range**: Moderate sensitivity
- **Entry-level**: Highly rate-sensitive, payment shock concerns

### Potential Scenarios
- **If rates drop to 5.5%**: Accumulated demand would rush back, inventory wiped out, new price acceleration
- **If rates rise**: Further cooling likely, especially in affordable segments

## Condo Market Specifics

### Pricing
- **Seattle median condo**: $550K approximate
- **Downtown high-rises (Belltown)**: $807K per unit (record)
- **South Lake Union**: $432K median (down 37%)

### Market Dynamics
- **First entry point**: Often first step for buyers
- **Most pressure**: Flat to negative price movement (-0.42% Seattle condos)
- **Rate sensitivity**: Most affected by high interest rates
- **Condition matters**: Newer buildings move faster
- **HOA scrutiny**: High buyer attention to fees and rules
- **Longer sales times**: Compared to single-family homes

## Rent vs Buy Analysis

### Rent Levels
- **Remain high**: Especially for family-sized housing
- **Downtown Oakland comparison**: Relatively affordable due to pre-pandemic construction boom

### Buy vs Rent Decision
- **Current environment**: Buying costs more monthly upfront
- **Long-term system**: Converts housing expense to equity building
- **No automatic answer**: Depends on personal timeline and circumstances
- **5+ year horizon**: Buying makes more sense
- **Mortgage vs rent**: Many would spend more in mortgage than rent at current rates

## Seasonal Patterns

### "New Year Kickoff" Phase
- **Timing**: January-March post-holidays
- **Characteristics**: Strong demand, limited inventory, competitive mid-price range
- **Buyer activity**: Prepared buyers stepping in early
- **Seller advantage**: Those pricing to current market winning
- **Historical pattern**: Repeats yearly per market experts

### Other Seasons
- **Spring/Summer**: Traditional hot season
- **Fall**: Activity remains but competitive
- **Winter**: Slowest but still active in Seattle

## Long-Term Investment Outlook

### Strengths
- **Tech industry base**: Resilient employment
- **Limited land**: Geography constrains new construction
- **Quality of life**: Nature proximity, culture, lifestyle appeal
- **Population growth**: Continued in-migration of skilled workers

### Risks
- **Affordability ceiling**: Limiting pool of qualified buyers
- **Tech sector volatility**: Economic base concentration risk
- **Remote work trends**: Could reduce demand if sustained
- **Climate change**: Increasing heat, fire risk (though moderate currently)

### Best Bets
- **5+ year hold**: Recommended minimum for buying
- **Modest appreciation expected**: Not explosive growth, but stable
- **Premium neighborhoods**: Most insulated from volatility
- **Eastside markets**: Strong fundamentals, excellent schools, tech proximity
