# Los Angeles Housing Market Knowledge

## Market Overview (2025)

### Median Home Prices
- **Los Angeles city**: $1.0M-$1.1M median
- **Redfin December 2025**: $1.0M (up 2.0% year-over-year)
- **Zillow**: $970,592 (down 0.5% year-over-year)
- **Los Angeles County**: $909,500-$942,610
- **LA Metro single-family**: $1.16M
- **Typical range**: $876K-$1.1M depending on source/property type

### Market Characteristics
- **Days on market**: 22-67 days (hot homes 22 days, average 67 days)
- **Sale-to-list price**: Hot homes sell 2% above list, average properties slightly below
- **Market status**: Somewhat competitive, more balanced than recent years
- **Inventory**: 15,000+ active listings countywide, improving from prior years
- **Competition**: Half of homes selling below asking price

### Price Comparison to National Average
- **LA median**: 127% higher than US national average ($398,400)
- **Cost of living**: 50-52% higher than national average

## Market Dynamics

### Current State (2025)
- **Price movement**: Flat to low single-digit gains year-over-year
- **Sales volume**: Mixed (LA Metro up 23.5% month-over-month in March, down 15.9% year-over-year November)
- **Market type**: Shift from seller frenzy to strategic opportunity
- **Buyer leverage**: Increasing, especially mid-tier homes
- **Listing time**: Extended compared to 2022-2023 peak

### Inventory
- **Active listings**: 15,000+ (Los Angeles County)
- **Trend**: Improving supply, still below balanced market threshold
- **Months of inventory**: Approaching balanced levels (4-6 months ideal)
- **New listings**: Seasonal variations (higher summer, lower winter)

### Competition Level
- **Premium homes**: Still competitive, multiple offers
- **Mid-tier**: More balanced, longer marketing time
- **Condos**: Surging inventory, buyer's market emerging
- **Turnkey vs dated**: Quality homes move much faster

## Challenges & Risks

### Affordability Crisis
- **Major issue**: Persistent housing shortage
- **High prices**: Limiting buyer pool
- **Income requirements**: $200K+ for median home
- **Displacement concerns**: Gentrification pressures

### Natural Hazards
- **Wildfires**: Edge zones most affected
- **Insurance**: Rising costs, availability issues
- **Earthquakes**: Seismic risk throughout region
- **Mitigation**: Shop coverage early, document steps

### Regulatory Environment
- **Zoning**: Restrictive in many areas
- **Permits**: Can be lengthy process
- **Rent control**: Affects condo conversion economics
- **Prop 13**: Tax implications
