# Market Timing and DOM Analysis

## Days on Market (DOM) Deep Dive

### Why DOM Matters

Days on market is the market's real-time verdict on pricing:
- Low DOM = property priced right or underpriced
- High DOM = property overpriced, issues, or weak market

### DOM Interpretation by Market Type

#### In a Seller's Market (low inventory)

| DOM | Interpretation | Price Prediction |
|-----|----------------|------------------|
| 0-3 days | Extremely hot, multiple offers | +5% to +10% above list |
| 4-7 days | Very strong demand | +2% to +5% above list |
| 8-14 days | Good demand | At list or slightly above |
| 15-30 days | Normal pace | At or slightly below list |
| 31-45 days | Slower than typical | -3% to -5% below list |
| > 45 days | Something wrong | -5% to -10%+ below list |

#### In a Balanced Market

| DOM | Interpretation | Price Prediction |
|-----|----------------|------------------|
| 0-7 days | Hot property | +2% to +5% above list |
| 8-21 days | Strong interest | At list price |
| 22-45 days | Normal pace | At or -2% below list |
| 46-60 days | Slowing | -3% to -6% below list |
| 61-90 days | Stale | -5% to -10% below list |
| > 90 days | Serious issues | -10%+ below list |

#### In a Buyer's Market (high inventory)

| DOM | Interpretation | Price Prediction |
|-----|----------------|------------------|
| 0-14 days | Strong property | At list |
| 15-30 days | Good | -2% to -4% below list |
| 31-60 days | Normal for market | -4% to -7% below list |
| 61-90 days | Needs price cut | -7% to -12% below list |
| > 90 days | Overpriced or issues | -12%+ below list |

### Price Reduction History

Look for signals in DOM context:
- **Multiple reductions**: Seller chasing market down, may continue
- **Recent reduction**: New price may be more realistic
- **No reduction despite high DOM**: Stubborn seller, more room to negotiate
- **Original price far above current**: Market has spoken

## Seasonal Market Patterns

### Spring Market (March - May)
- **Peak activity**: Inventory rises, buyers most active
- **Price pressure**: Upward, competition increases
- **DOM impact**: Low DOM means bidding wars likely
- **Prediction adjustment**: +1% to +2% for well-priced homes

### Summer Market (June - August)
- **Activity**: High but plateauing
- **Family pressure**: Need to close before school year
- **DOM impact**: Homes listed in late summer may sit longer
- **Prediction adjustment**: +0% to +1%, declining toward August

### Fall Market (September - November)
- **Activity**: Declining from peak
- **Seller motivation**: Get out before holidays/winter
- **DOM impact**: DOM tolerance higher, but motivated sellers
- **Prediction adjustment**: -1% to 0%

### Winter Market (December - February)
- **Activity**: Lowest of year, holiday lull
- **Listings**: Fewer, often motivated sellers
- **DOM impact**: High DOM less meaningful, market is slow
- **Prediction adjustment**: -2% to -3%, but serious sellers may deal

## Interest Rate Impact

### Current Rate Environment Effects

| Rate Change (90 days) | Market Impact | Price Adjustment |
|----------------------|---------------|------------------|
| Dropped 0.5%+ | Buyers rushing in | +2% to +3% |
| Dropped 0.25% | Positive sentiment | +1% |
| Stable | Neutral | 0% |
| Rose 0.25% | Slight cooling | -1% |
| Rose 0.5%+ | Significant cooling | -2% to -3% |
| Rose 1%+ | Market shock | -3% to -5% |

### Affordability Impact

Every 1% rate increase reduces buying power by ~10%:
- 6% rate: $400k home = $2,398/mo P&I
- 7% rate: $400k home = $2,661/mo P&I (+11%)
- 8% rate: $400k home = $2,935/mo P&I (+22% vs 6%)

In rising rate environments:
- Buyers qualify for less
- Monthly payment sensitivity increases
- Price competition decreases
- DOM typically increases

## Integrating Market Signals

### Bullish Signals (predict above list)
- DOM < 14 in any market
- Multiple similar homes sold above list recently
- Rate environment improving
- Spring market, low inventory
- Price reduction brought to fair value

### Bearish Signals (predict below list)
- DOM > 45 in any market
- Recent comparable sales below list
- Rising rates reducing affordability
- Winter market, high inventory
- Price still above recent comps

### Mixed Signals (wider prediction range)
- DOM 15-45, unclear direction
- Market transitioning (balance shifting)
- Conflicting comparable sales
- Unusual property type
- Major economic uncertainty
