# LLC Registration Guide for Perchspot

## Why Form an LLC?

An LLC (Limited Liability Company) provides:
- **Personal asset protection** - Separates personal assets from business liabilities
- **Tax flexibility** - Choose pass-through taxation or corporate taxation
- **Credibility** - Professional business structure for customers and partners
- **Simplicity** - Less paperwork than corporations

---

## LLC Registration Process (7 Steps)

### Step 1: Choose a Business Name
- Must be unique and distinguishable in your state
- Check availability with your state's Secretary of State
- Typically must include "LLC" or "Limited Liability Company"
- For Perchspot: "Perchspot LLC" or "Perchspot, LLC"

### Step 2: Choose a State to Register

| Option | Filing Fee | Annual Fee | Pros | Cons |
|--------|-----------|------------|------|------|
| **Home State** | Varies | Varies | Simple, no foreign registration | State-specific rules |
| **Wyoming** | $100 | $60 | Lowest costs, no state income tax, strong privacy | May need foreign registration in home state |
| **Delaware** | $110 | $300 | Best for VC/investors, business-friendly courts | Higher annual fee |
| **California** | $70 | $800 | N/A if you live there | Highest annual tax |

**Recommendation**: Register in your home state unless you have specific reasons (investors, privacy, tax optimization).

### Step 3: Appoint a Registered Agent
- Required in all states
- Receives legal documents on behalf of your LLC
- Options:
  - **Yourself** - Free, but address becomes public record
  - **Registered agent service** - $50-300/year, provides privacy

### Step 4: File Articles of Organization
- Filed with Secretary of State
- Contains: LLC name, registered agent, member names, business purpose
- Processing time: 7-10 days (varies by state)

### Step 5: Create an Operating Agreement
- Defines ownership structure, profit distribution, management
- Not required in most states, but **highly recommended**
- Essential if you have partners or seek investors

### Step 6: Get an EIN (Employer Identification Number)
- **Free** from IRS (Form SS-4 or online at irs.gov)
- Required for:
  - Opening business bank account
  - Hiring employees
  - Filing taxes

### Step 7: Open a Business Bank Account
- Keeps personal and business finances separate
- Maintains liability protection
- Required: EIN, Articles of Organization, Operating Agreement

## Recommended Approach for Perchspot

### Option A: California LLC (If Based in CA)
- **Pros**: Simple, no foreign registration needed
- **Cons**: $800/year franchise tax
- **Best if**: You live/operate primarily in California

### Option B: Wyoming LLC + CA Foreign Registration
- **Pros**: Lower ongoing costs if expanding to multiple states
- **Cons**: Need to file in both states initially
- **Best if**: Privacy important, planning multi-state operations

### Option C: Delaware LLC (Future VC)
- **Pros**: Investor-friendly, established case law
- **Cons**: Higher annual fee ($300), may need foreign registration
- **Best if**: Planning to raise venture capital

**Recommendation for Solo SaaS**: Start with your home state. The complexity of out-of-state registration rarely justifies the savings for a single-member LLC.
