## 3. Long Description

```
🏠 **The Problem**
Buying a home is the biggest purchase most people make, yet buyers rely on agents with conflicting incentives and Zillow estimates that miss critical details. You're supposed to spend $500K+ based on a 30-minute tour and gut feeling?

🎯 **What Perchspot Does**
Enter any property address. In under 5 minutes, get a comprehensive AI analysis covering:

• **Property Condition** — Age, maintenance outlook, systems, and red flags
• **Schools** — Ratings, distance, and quality breakdown for nearby schools
• **Location** — Walkability, transit access, neighborhood safety, amenities
• **Investment** — Market trends, price history, rental potential, appreciation outlook

Each category gets a clear 0-100 score so you can compare properties objectively.

💡 **Why It's Different**
- **Unbiased**: No commission, no sales pitch—just data
- **Instant**: Reports in minutes, not days of research
- **Affordable**: $3-5 per report (cheaper than your coffee)
- **Ask Questions**: Chat with AI about anything in the report

Built by a software engineer and his real estate agent wife who got tired of watching buyers make $50,000 mistakes because they missed obvious signals.

Perfect for first-time buyers, investors, or anyone who wants to know what they're really getting before making an offer.
```

## 9. Launch Day Engagement

### Do's
- Respond to every comment within 30 minutes
- Thank people for feedback genuinely
- Share behind-the-scenes updates
- Post milestone updates ("Just hit 100 upvotes!")
- Be present all day (it matters)

### Don'ts
- Don't ask for upvotes directly (against PH rules)
- Don't spam communities
- Don't ignore critical feedback
- Don't disappear after posting

### Sample Responses

**To positive feedback**:
```
Thanks so much! Really appreciate you checking it out. If you try a report, I'd love to hear what you think of the [specific feature].
```

**To feature requests**:
```
Great idea! We've actually been thinking about this. Adding it to our roadmap. Would you want [specific implementation] or something different?
```

**To criticism**:
```
Fair point—thanks for the honest feedback. We're working on [addressing concern]. Would love to have you try it again once we ship that update.
```
