# Real Estate Investment Metrics Analysis

## Top 10 Investment Performance Indicators

### 1. Net Operating Income (NOI)
**Definition**: Total income minus operating expenses (excluding mortgage payments)

**Formula**: `NOI = Total Income - Operating Expenses`

**Components**:
- Income: Rent + fees (laundry, parking, service)
- Operating Expenses: Property management, legal, maintenance, property taxes, utilities
- EXCLUDED: Capital expenditures, income taxes, mortgage payments, interest

**Analysis Guidelines**:
- Positive NOI: Property generates enough to cover mortgage
- Negative NOI: Major red flag, unsustainable
- Compare to similar properties in area
- Calculate as % of property value (should be 3-8%)

### 2. Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate)
**Formula**: `Cap Rate = NOI / Property Value`

**Interpretation**:
- **8-12%**: Higher cap rate = Higher risk, higher returns (emerging markets)
- **4-7%**: Moderate cap rate = Balanced risk/return (established suburbs)
- **2-4%**: Lower cap rate = Lower risk, stable markets (NYC, SF, Seattle)

**Analysis Guidelines**:
- Compare to local market average (varies by location)
- Higher cap rate properties may need more active management
- Lower cap rate often indicates appreciation potential
- Typical range: 4-8% for residential

### 3. Cash Flow
**Formula**: `Cash Flow = Total Rental Income - All Expenses (including mortgage)`

**Interpretation**:
- Positive cash flow: Sustainable investment
- Negative cash flow: "Cash flow negative" - risky unless expecting appreciation
- Monthly target: $200-500+ per property minimum

**Analysis Guidelines**:
- Calculate monthly and annual cash flow
- Factor in 5-10% vacancy rate
- Include maintenance reserves (1% of property value annually)
- Positive cash flow required for long-term rental strategy

### 4. Cash-on-Cash Return
**Formula**: `CoC Return = Annual Net Cash Flow / Total Cash Invested × 100%`

**Total Cash Invested** = Down payment + Closing costs + Initial repairs

**Interpretation**:
- **12-20%+**: Excellent return
- **8-12%**: Good return
- **4-8%**: Acceptable return
- **<4%**: Below average, may not justify risks

**Analysis Guidelines**:
- More important than cap rate for leveraged properties
- Accounts for financing impact
- Compare to alternative investments (stocks typically 7-10%)
- Target minimum: 8% for rental properties

### 5. Gross Rent Multiplier (GRM)
**Formula**: `GRM = Property Price / Annual Gross Rental Income`

**Interpretation**:
- **4-7**: Good value (lower is better)
- **8-11**: Fair value
- **12-15**: Overpriced for rental investment
- **15+**: Not viable as rental

**Analysis Guidelines**:
- Quick screening tool, not comprehensive
- Compare to local market average
- Does NOT account for expenses or vacancies
- Use for initial filtering, then deeper analysis

### 6. Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
**Definition**: Estimated annual growth rate over holding period

**Typical Range**: 10-20% (varies by asset type)

**Analysis Guidelines**:
- Accounts for time value of money
- Includes appreciation, cash flow, and equity buildup
- Best for comparing similar properties
- Assumes stable rental environment
- Calculate using Excel IRR function with projected 5-10 year cash flows

### 7. Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
**Formula**: `DSCR = NOI / Total Debt Service`

**Interpretation**:
- **DSCR > 1.25**: Excellent - strong income cushion
- **DSCR 1.1-1.25**: Good - adequate coverage
- **DSCR 1.0-1.1**: Marginal - tight margins
- **DSCR < 1.0**: Insufficient income to cover debt

**Analysis Guidelines**:
- Lenders typically require minimum 1.20-1.25
- Higher DSCR = easier financing and lower risk
- Factor in when evaluating financing options

### 8. Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV)
**Formula**: `LTV = Loan Amount / Property Fair Market Value × 100%`

**Typical Lender Limits**:
- Primary residence: Up to 97% (with PMI)
- Investment property: 75-80% maximum
- Commercial: 65-75% maximum

**Analysis Guidelines**:
- Lower LTV = more equity, less risk
- Higher LTV = more leverage, higher potential returns
- Target 70-80% LTV for good balance
- Affects cash-on-cash return significantly

### 9. Return on Investment (ROI)
**Formula**: `ROI = (Gain - Cost) / Cost × 100%`

**Analysis Guidelines**:
- Comprehensive measure including appreciation
- Calculate over 5, 10, and 30-year periods
- Include all gains: cash flow, appreciation, tax benefits, loan paydown
- Compare to alternative investments

### 10. Occupancy Rate
**Formula**: `Occupancy Rate = (Rented Units / Total Units) × 100%`

**Benchmarks**:
- **95-100%**: Excellent management
- **90-95%**: Good, typical for well-managed properties
- **85-90%**: Acceptable, room for improvement
- **<85%**: Poor, indicates problems

**Analysis Guidelines**:
- Affects NOI and cash flow directly
- Factor in seasonal variations
- High vacancy = pricing too high or poor property condition
- Low vacancy (<97%) = may be underpriced

## Market Indicators to Consider

### Local Market Factors
- Employment rates and major employers
- Population growth trends (2%+ annual growth is strong)
- New construction activity
- Rental rate trends (3-5% annual increases ideal)
- Economic development plans

### Property-Specific Factors
- Age and condition (newer = lower maintenance)
- Required capital expenditures
- Property management quality
- Tenant quality and lease terms
- Appreciation potential (compare to historical data)

## Investment Strategy Analysis

### Buy and Hold (Rental)
**Key Metrics**: Cash flow, NOI, occupancy rate, cap rate
**Target**: Positive monthly cash flow + 8%+ CoC return
**Best for**: Stable, long-term income generation

### Fix and Flip
**Key Metrics**: Purchase price, renovation costs, ARV (After Repair Value), holding period
**Target**: 20-30% ROI in 3-12 months
**Best for**: Active investors with construction experience

### Primary Residence
**Key Metrics**: Price/income ratio, total monthly costs, appreciation potential
**Target**: Housing costs <28% of gross income
**Best for**: Long-term ownership with lifestyle considerations

## Analysis Framework

For any investment property, calculate and report:

1. **Income Potential**
   - Estimated monthly rent
   - NOI (annual)
   - Gross rental yield

2. **Return Metrics**
   - Cap rate
   - Cash-on-cash return
   - Projected 5-year IRR

3. **Financial Health**
   - Monthly cash flow
   - DSCR
   - GRM compared to market

4. **Risk Assessment**
   - LTV ratio
   - Vacancy risk
   - Market appreciation trends

5. **Comparison**
   - How metrics compare to local market averages
   - Better/worse than similar properties
   - Overall investment grade (A, B, C, D)

## Investment Grade Scale

- **A (Excellent)**: Cap rate 6%+, CoC 12%+, DSCR 1.3+, strong market
- **B (Good)**: Cap rate 5-6%, CoC 8-12%, DSCR 1.2-1.3, stable market
- **C (Fair)**: Cap rate 4-5%, CoC 5-8%, DSCR 1.1-1.2, average market
- **D (Poor)**: Cap rate <4%, CoC <5%, DSCR <1.1, weak market
