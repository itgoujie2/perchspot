# Sale Price Adjustment Factors

## Feature-Based Adjustments

### Property Characteristics

| Feature | Above Average | At Average | Below Average |
|---------|---------------|------------|---------------|
| Beds/Baths | +1% (extra bath adds value) | 0% | -1% (fewer than typical) |
| Sqft vs Area | +1% to +3% (larger) | 0% | -1% to -3% (smaller) |
| Lot Size (suburb) | +1% to +3% (larger lot) | 0% | -1% to -2% (small lot) |
| Garage | +1% to +2% (extra spaces) | 0% | -2% (no garage in car area) |

### Age & Condition

| Condition | Adjustment | Notes |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| New construction | +3% to +5% | Warranty, modern features |
| Built < 10 years | +2% to +3% | Recent, minimal deferred maintenance |
| Built 10-30 years | +0% to +1% | Standard, age-appropriate wear |
| Built 30-50 years | -1% to -2% | May need updates, older systems |
| Built > 50 years | -2% to -4% | Unless renovated or historic |
| Recently renovated | +2% to +4% | Updated systems, modern finishes |
| Original/dated | -2% to -4% | 80s kitchens, brass fixtures |
| Deferred maintenance | -3% to -6% | Visible issues, roof age, etc. |

### Location Quality

| Factor | Premium | Neutral | Discount |
|--------|---------|---------|----------|
| School ratings | +2% (top schools) | 0% | -2% (low ratings) |
| Walk score | +2% to +4% (urban, >80) | 0% | -1% (suburban, <40) |
| Crime | 0% (low crime) | 0% | -3% to -5% (high crime) |
| Noise (traffic/trains) | 0% | -1% (moderate) | -3% to -5% (severe) |
| Views | +2% to +5% (water/city/mountain) | 0% | -1% (power lines, highway) |

### Financial Factors

| Factor | Impact | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| HOA < $200/mo | 0% | Reasonable |
| HOA $200-$400/mo | -1% | Typical condo/townhome |
| HOA $400-$600/mo | -2% | High, check amenities |
| HOA > $600/mo | -3% to -5% | Very high, buyer concern |
| Special assessments pending | -2% to -4% | Known future costs |
| Low property taxes | +1% | Lower carrying costs |
| High property taxes (>1.5%) | -1% to -2% | Higher carrying costs |

## Market-Based Adjustments

### Price Position vs Estimate

| List vs Redfin Estimate | Adjustment | Notes |
|-------------------------|------------|-------|
| List 10%+ below estimate | -2% (already discounted) | May sell closer to list |
| List 5-10% below estimate | -1% | Priced to sell |
| List within 5% of estimate | 0% | Fair pricing |
| List 5-10% above estimate | +0% (expect negotiation) | Price may come down |
| List 10%+ above estimate | +0% to -5% | Likely overpriced, long DOM expected |

### Comparable Sales Context

| Comparable Signal | Adjustment | Notes |
|-------------------|------------|-------|
| Recent sales above list | +2% to +4% | Hot micro-market |
| Recent sales at/near list | 0% | Balanced |
| Recent sales below list | -2% to -4% | Cooling market |
| No recent comparables | Widen range | Less confidence |

## Combining Adjustments

### Maximum Total Adjustment
- Cap positive adjustments at +15% above baseline
- Cap negative adjustments at -20% below baseline
- Beyond these ranges, question the baseline

### Priority of Factors
1. **Days on market** - strongest signal for current pricing reality
2. **Market conditions** - macro environment affects all properties
3. **Price vs estimate spread** - immediate pricing accuracy signal
4. **Property condition** - visible issues trump other factors
5. **Location factors** - schools, walkability matter for buyers
6. **Financial factors** - HOA, taxes affect monthly payment

### Adjustment Confidence
- 1-3 major factors: high confidence in adjustments
- 4-6 factors: medium confidence
- 7+ factors: consider widening range, too many variables
