# Bay Area / San Francisco Housing Market Knowledge

## San Francisco City Market Overview (2025)

### Median Home Prices
- **San Francisco citywide (all properties)**: $1.35M-$1.4M
- **Single-family homes**: $1.7M-$1.8M median
- **Condos**: $1.15M median
- **Redfin December 2025**: $1.4M (up 2.2% year-over-year)

### Market Characteristics
- **Days on market**: 36 days (down from 43 days previous year)
- **Sale-to-list price**: Hot homes sell 12% above list price
- **Pending time**: 15 days for competitive properties
- **2025 sales**: 399 homes (up from 343 previous year, +16.3%)
- **Competition level**: Very high in premium neighborhoods

### Price Comparison to National Average
- **San Francisco median**: 203% higher than US national average
- **Cost of living**: 64% higher than national average

### Market Momentum
- **2024**: Banner year, sellers' market
- **2025**: Market "thawing" after yearslong freeze
- **AI boom impact**: Driving new wave of wealthy buyers
- **Demand**: Swelling but unevenly distributed by neighborhood and price point

## San Francisco Neighborhood Prices

### Ultra-Premium ($2M-$5M+ Median)
**Pacific Heights**:
- **Typical range**: $2M-$5M+
- **2025 movement**: Down 40.6% year-over-year (significant correction)
- **Sale-to-list**: 1% below list price
- **Days on market**: 40 days (vs 62 prior year)
- **Status**: Still very competitive despite correction
- **Characteristics**: Grand Victorian homes, bay/bridge views, safe, great parks

**Marina**:
- **Range**: $2M-$5M+
- **Characteristics**: Waterfront proximity, young professionals, restaurants

**Russian Hill, Nob Hill, Telegraph Hill**:
- **Premium**: $2M+ typical
- **Characteristics**: Iconic San Francisco neighborhoods, views, cable cars

## Market Dynamics by Price Segment

### Luxury ($2M+)
- **AI boom beneficiaries**: Tech workers with stock gains
- **OpenAI/Anthropic IPO potential**: Could add "rocket fuel" to market in 2026
- **Supply constraint**: New listings declining despite hot market
- **Seller behavior**: Waiting for prices to rise more or rates to drop
- **Competition**: Intense for turnkey properties
- **Pacific Heights specifics**: Despite 40% drop, still very competitive

### Upper-Middle ($1M-$2M)
- **Tech professional target**: Dual-income tech households
- **Condition critical**: Updated homes significantly outperform dated ones
- **Negotiation leverage**: More than luxury, less than entry-level

### Entry-Level (Under $1M - Mostly Condos)
- **Condo market**: Taking especially long to sell
- **Rent vs buy equation**: Often cheaper to rent at current rates
- **Downtown Oakland**: Affordable rents due to pre-pandemic construction boom
- **Single-family scarcity**: Very limited options under $1M

## Inventory Dynamics

### Unsold Inventory Index (UII)
**Definition**: Months to sell existing inventory if no new listings

**Current Levels** (October 2025):
- **San Francisco**: 1.2 months (extreme seller's market)
- **San Mateo**: 1.7 months
- **Santa Clara**: 1.5 months
- **Alameda**: 2.0 months
- **Contra Costa**: 2.4 months
- **California statewide**: 3.2 months

**Interpretation**:
- Under 4 months = seller's market
- Under 2 months = extreme seller's market
- Bay Area severely constrained

### Active Listings Trend
- **Increasing**: Improvement from prior years
- **Still low**: Not keeping pace with demand
- **New listings declining**: Despite market heating up
- **Land constraints**: Limited new construction possible in SF

## Rent vs Buy Analysis

### Current Economics
- **Mortgage payments**: Often exceed rent at current rates/prices
- **Incentive**: Many choosing to keep renting
- **Exception**: Those with significant down payments or cash

### Income Requirements
- **San Francisco $1.35M median**: Requires $340K-$400K annual income with 20% down
- **Down payment needed**: $270,000 (20%)
- **Monthly costs**: $9,000-$10,500 including 0.72% property tax
- **Reality**: Often dual-income tech households required

## Unique Bay Area Factors

### Geography & Constraints
- **Peninsula**: San Francisco on peninsula limits expansion
- **Bay**: Water on multiple sides restricts growth
- **Hills**: Topography limits buildable land
- **Seismic**: Earthquake considerations affect construction

### Regulatory Environment
- **Strict zoning**: Limits density in many areas
- **Environmental review**: Slows new construction
- **Rent control**: Affects condo conversion economics
- **Proposition 13**: Property tax increase caps after purchase

### Cultural Factors
- **Progressive politics**: Affects housing policy
- **NIMBY resistance**: Neighborhood opposition to development
- **Historic preservation**: Limits demolition/redevelopment
- **Quality of life**: Nature proximity highly valued

## School Districts

### Top-Rated Areas (Driving Premium Prices)
- **Palo Alto Unified**: Extreme premium, top scores
- **San Ramon Valley USD**: Excellent, more affordable than Peninsula
- **Piedmont USD**: Small district, top-rated, very expensive

### San Francisco Public Schools
- **Variation**: Wide quality range within city
- **Assignment system**: Complex lottery system
- **Private school prevalence**: Many families use private schools
- **Impact**: Less school-driven premium than suburbs

## Risk Factors

### Economic
- **Tech sector concentration**: Vulnerability to tech downturn
- **Wealth inequality**: Market dependent on top earners
- **Affordability crisis**: Long-term sustainability questions

### Natural Hazards
- **Earthquakes**: Major seismic risk (San Andreas, Hayward faults)
- **Wildfires**: Increasing risk in North Bay, East Bay hills
- **Sea level rise**: Affects some waterfront areas
- **Drought**: Water supply concerns

### Policy & Social
- **Homelessness**: Quality of life issues in some areas
- **Crime concerns**: Varies greatly by neighborhood
- **Tax burden**: High state/local taxes
- **Regulation**: Business environment challenges

## Investment Outlook

### Strengths
- **Tech innovation hub**: Global center of tech industry
- **Wealth concentration**: High-earning population base
- **Limited supply**: Geography constrains inventory
- **Quality of life**: Nature, culture, climate appeal
- **World-class universities**: Stanford, UC Berkeley talent pipeline

### Best Investment Areas
- **Long-term hold**: 10+ years recommended
- **Premium neighborhoods**: Most insulated from volatility
- **Peninsula**: Closest to tech job centers
- **Established submarkets**: Proven appreciation history

### Caution Areas
- **Entry-level condos**: Oversupply in some segments
- **Marginal neighborhoods**: Higher volatility
- **Remote suburbs**: Vulnerable to WFH trend reversals
