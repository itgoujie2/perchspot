# Photo Analysis Guide

## What to Look For in Property Photos

### Exterior Assessment
- **Roof condition**: Look for sagging, missing shingles, moss buildup
- **Siding**: Check for cracks, warping, paint condition
- **Foundation**: Look for visible cracks or settling
- **Landscaping**: Overall maintenance level

### Interior Assessment
- **Kitchen**: Appliances age, counter condition, cabinet quality
- **Bathrooms**: Fixtures age, tile condition, water damage signs
- **Flooring**: Type, condition, consistency throughout
- **Walls/Ceilings**: Paint quality, cracks, water stains

### Red Flags
- Heavy staging/furniture (hiding issues)
- Odd camera angles (hiding problems)
- Missing key rooms (bathrooms, kitchen)
- Very few photos (< 10 for a house)

## Scoring Guidance
- Modern updates (< 5 years): +10 points
- Well-maintained: +5 points
- Deferred maintenance visible: -10 points
- Major issues visible: -20 points
