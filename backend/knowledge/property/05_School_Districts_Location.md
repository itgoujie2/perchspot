# School Districts & Location Factors Knowledge

## School District Impact on Home Values

### Price Premium Statistics
- **Homes near top-rated schools (9-10 rating)**: 78%+ higher than surrounding area
- **National median for highest-rated districts**: $1.21 million (125% premium over metro average in 2025)
- **High-performing suburban districts**: 49% higher than national median
- **Comparison**: 77% more expensive than homes in low-ranked districts
- **Test score improvement**: 5% increase correlates with 2.5% home value increase
- **School spending**: Every $1 spent on public schools increases home values by $20
- **Rating tier impact**: Homes in districts rated 8-10 command highest markups

### Value Appreciation Patterns
- **Top-rated districts (8+ rating)**: 15-25% faster appreciation over 5 years compared to average districts
- **Market resilience**: Homes in strong districts hold value better during economic downturns
- **Long-term stability**: Property values in high-performing districts show steadier gains over time
- **Depreciation resistance**: Properties in top-tier zones depreciate less than lower-rated districts

## Market Dynamics by School Rating Tier

### Top Tier (Ratings 9-10)
**Price Impact**: Highest premium, $1.21M median (125% above metro average)

**Market Characteristics**:
- Intense bidding wars common
- Homes sell approximately 8 days faster than average
- Limited inventory
- Strong competition from families with children

**Buyer Profile**:
- Families prioritizing education
- High-income households
- Long-term residents planning to stay

---

### High-Performing Tier (Rating 8)
**Price Impact**: Moderate premium but better value

**Advantages**:
- Engaged communities
- Well-maintained properties
- Solid appreciation potential
- Less intense competition than 9-10 tier

**Best For**: Buyers seeking balance between school quality and value, avoiding bidding wars

---

### Moderate Tier (Ratings 5-7)
**Price Impact**: At or slightly above metro median

**Buyer Profile**:
- First-time homeowners
- Investors seeking rental properties
- Families planning private schools
- Those willing to trade off school quality for lower price

**Opportunity**: Districts showing improvement (new construction, rising test scores) may capture appreciation as ratings climb

---

### Lower Tier (Ratings 1-4)
**Price Impact**: Below metro median, longer selling times

**Characteristics**:
- Decreased demand
- Lower property values
- Slower sales
- Less competitive market

## School District Redistricting Impact

### Boundary Changes
- Can significantly affect property values
- Moving from lower-rated to higher-rated district increases value
- Moving from higher-rated to lower-rated district decreases value
- Often controversial community issue

### Example (from provided context)
- Somerset (Bellevue): Borderline properties moved from Issaquah School District to Bellevue School District
- Significant impact on market desirability
- Property values directly affected by district assignment

### Monitoring
- School board meetings and announcements
- Long-range facilities plans
- Enrollment projections
- Redistricting proposals

## Property Tax Considerations

### Higher Taxes in Strong Districts
- Property taxes correlate with home values
- Higher-valued homes = higher property taxes
- Property taxes often directly fund local schools
- Creates cycle: higher taxes → better-funded schools → increased home values → higher taxes

### Tax vs Private School Trade-off
- Average private school tuition (North Carolina example): $10,442/year
- Higher home price in strong district often cheaper long-term than private school
- Property tax increase may be less than private tuition
- Added benefit: property value appreciation

## Research Strategies

### Online Resources
- **GreatSchools.org**: Ratings, test scores, reviews
- **Niche.com**: Comprehensive ratings, parent reviews, cultural fit information
- **State education websites**: Official performance data
- **Realtor.com/Zillow**: School information integrated in listings
- **Local newspaper archives**: District news and trends

### In-Person Research
- **School visits**: Schedule tours, observe environment
- **Meet administrators**: Ask about programs, philosophy, resources
- **Attend events**: Open houses, school board meetings
- **Talk to residents**: Current parent experiences invaluable
- **Visit at different times**: Morning drop-off, afternoon pickup to assess traffic/activity

### Questions to Ask
- Teacher retention rates
- Class sizes and student-to-teacher ratios
- Safety protocols and track record
- Academic program offerings (AP, honors, special ed)
- Extracurricular opportunities
- Technology integration
- College acceptance rates
- District financial health and budget trends

## Magnet Schools and Charter Schools

### Alternative Options
- **Magnet schools**: Public schools with specialized curricula (STEM, arts, language)
- **Charter schools**: Publicly funded but independently operated
- **Benefits**: Can provide excellent education without premium neighborhood costs
- **Consideration**: May require applications, lotteries, or transportation planning

### Impact on Home Values
- Availability of quality alternatives may reduce premium for traditional district boundaries
- Homes near highly-rated magnet schools still command premiums
- Charter school quality varies significantly

---

## Proximity to Schools

### Walking Distance Premium
- Homes within walking distance of highly-rated elementary schools particularly valuable
- Studies show positive correlation between proximity and property values
- Convenience factor for families

### Potential Drawbacks of Close Proximity
- **Traffic**: Drop-off/pickup congestion twice daily
- **Noise**: Increased activity during school hours, outdoor activities
- **Parking**: Limited street parking during events

### Optimal Strategy (for non-families)
- Within district boundaries but not immediately adjacent
- Gain financial benefits without daily disruption
- Maintain resale value advantages

## Long-Term Investment Perspective

### 5-Year Outlook
- Strong districts: Expect continued demand and appreciation
- Improving districts: Potential for outsized gains as ratings improve
- Declining districts: Risk of value stagnation or decline

### 10+ Year Outlook
- School quality tends to remain stable (districts rarely drastically change)
- Demographics shifts can impact enrollment and funding
- New school construction can improve district appeal
- Administrative changes can affect performance

### Resale Considerations
- Buyer pool larger in strong districts
- Homes sell faster (average 8 days sooner)

## School District Red Flags

### Warning Signs
- Declining enrollment
- Budget cuts and teacher layoffs
- Aging, poorly maintained facilities
- High teacher turnover
- Falling test scores or ratings
- Administrative instability
- Negative news coverage
- Parent complaints and protests

### Due Diligence
- Review 3-5 year trends (enrollment, test scores, budgets)
- Check local news for controversies
- Attend school board meeting
- Review district financial reports
- Talk to multiple current families

## District Spending Efficiency

### Spending ≠ Quality
- Some districts operate more efficiently (high quality, lower spending)
- Others spend more with less to show for it
- District size affects efficiency
- Proximity to neighboring districts affects perception

### Spending Patterns
- Wealthy districts often spend more efficiently
- Highest spending: low-income areas, large districts, fewer homes
- May not directly correlate with home values

## Best Practices for Home Buyers

### Priority Assessment
1. Define must-have school attributes
2. Set budget including property tax consideration
3. Research multiple districts
4. Visit schools and neighborhoods
5. Consider long-term plans (family, career, resale)

### Balance Factors
- Don't overpay solely for schools if planning to sell before children attend
- Consider total cost of ownership (home price + property taxes)
- Evaluate entire community, not just schools
- Assess personal priorities (commute, lifestyle, amenities)

### Future-Proofing
- Even without children, strong schools protect investment
- Easier to sell in strong districts during any market
- Broader buyer appeal
- Better appreciation potential
