# Austin Housing Market Knowledge

## Market Overview (2025-2026)

### Median Home Prices
- **Austin city**: $520K-$550K
- **Redfin December 2025**: $520K (down 2.8% year-over-year)
- **Zillow**: $512,937 (down 6.8% year-over-year)
- **Austin Metro (MSA)**: $430K-$449,900
- **Peak (mid-2022)**: $550K
- **Current vs peak**: Down 18-20% from 2022 peak

### County Breakdown (December 2025)
- **City of Austin**: $550,000 median
- **Travis County**: $512,500 (up 2.5% in some periods)
- **Williamson County**: Lower inventory (3.7 months)
- **Hays County**: Moderate
- **Bastrop County**: Growing, lower prices
- **Caldwell County**: $270,000 (lowest), down 14.6%, 7.7 months inventory

### Market Characteristics
- **Days on market**: 88-91 days average (highest since March 2011)
- **Sale-to-list price**: At or slightly below list price
- **Market status**: BUYER'S MARKET - significant correction ongoing
- **Inventory**: 4.2 months supply (balanced market), record highs
- **Months of inventory trend**: Up 1.5 months year-over-year

### Price Comparison to National Average
- **Austin median**: 17% higher than US national average ($398,400)
- **Cost of living**: 4% LOWER than national average
- **Comparison**: More affordable than major coastal cities

---

## Market Correction Context

### Peak to Present
- **2022 Peak**: $550K median
- **Current (2025)**: $430K-$520K depending on source/area
- **Decline**: 18-20% from peak
- **Type**: Normalization/correction, not crash
- **Duration**: Began 2023, continued through 2025

### Comparison to Other Texas Metros
- **Dallas**: Median $380,000 (modest 3-5% softening)
- **Houston**: Median $330,000 (modest 3-5% softening)
- **San Antonio**: Similar correction patterns to Austin
- **Austin's uniqueness**: Most extreme run-up during pandemic, therefore steepest correction

### Why Austin Corrected More
- **Pandemic appreciation**: 50-60% vs 30-40% other Texas metros
- **Investor activity**: Higher concentration of investor purchases during boom
- **Remote work reversal**: Some pandemic migrants returning to origin cities
- **Affordability ceiling**: Hit limit sooner than other metros
- **Property tax burden**: Among highest in Texas
